Why Hanwa

Why Hanwa for ESD Reliability Testing

Hanwa ESD testers are known for their low-parasitic architecture and highly repeatable measurement behavior, making them particularly well-suited for advanced semiconductor devices and high-pin-count applications.

Their design emphasizes waveform stability and predictability — critical when qualifying sensitive components, troubleshooting failures, or comparing reliability results across multiple programs.

Modern Hanwa platforms also support multiple ESD and latch-up evaluation modes within a single system, allowing laboratories to consolidate test capability without added operational complexity.

The Hanwa Product Line

One System Family, Every ESD Test Mode

From package-level ESD to wafer-level, CDM, TLP, and latch-up — Hanwa consolidates test capability into a single, qualification-ready platform family.

HED-G5000

Next-Gen ESD / Latch-Up Tester

Non-relay GNC module supporting up to 2,048 pins. Low-parasitic architecture for high-precision detection on advanced devices.

HED-S5000

Automated Package ESD Tester

Fully automated, integrated final-test system for production lines and research labs, with electrical, capacitance, and resistance test modes.

HED-W5000M

Wafer-Level ESD Tester

HBM & MM waveform testing at the wafer level. 0–±4kV charging (8kV option) with 1% ± 5V accuracy.

HED-C5000R

CDM Tester

Charged Device Model testing conforming to JS-002, JEDEC, ESDA, and AEC specifications — the recommended model of leading semiconductor companies.

HED-T5000

TLP Tester

Transmission Line Pulse testing for investigating device protection circuits and correlating results with ESD failure analysis.
